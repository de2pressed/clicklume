# Code of conduct

ClickLume contributors are expected to keep project spaces respectful,
constructive, and focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained
personal attacks are not acceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome when it
addresses evidence and design trade-offs rather than the person presenting it.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to
the repository owner. Reports will be handled with discretion and without
retaliation against good-faith reporters.
